# Blind Cricket Pro Ultimate

A screen-reader-friendly audio cricket game you can play in your browser.

## About the game

Play cricket against the computer with spoken and text commentary, sound effects, and clear controls. Choose a match format, bat or bowl, and build your cricket career. You can also set up an 11-player team and play offline tournaments against the computer.

## How to play

1. Open the game and choose **Continue with Google** to sign in.
2. Select **Play versus computer** or another match mode on the home screen.
3. Choose your match settings and start the match. Follow the on-screen and spoken instructions to bat or bowl.
4. If you leave an unfinished match, sign in with the same Google account and select **Resume Saved Match** on the home screen or in the Resume menu.

Commentary, text-to-speech, and volume options are available in Settings. A saved match may be stored on your device while its cloud checkpoint is still syncing.

## Features

- Matches versus the computer, team play, daily challenges, and offline tournaments.
- Commentary clips in English, Hindi, Urdu, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Bengali, with text and screen-reader announcements.
- An opt-in top-10 leaderboard.
- Friend challenges: each player makes one attempt against the computer, and their scores are compared.
- A shop for in-game items, with gift and sale offers to other players. Recipients accept or decline offers in the Shop.
- A feedback form for signed-in players to contact the game owner.
- A **Resume Saved Match** option for unfinished matches after signing in again.

## Play online

[Open Blind Cricket Pro Ultimate](https://choudharyshyam5-ctrl.github.io/Blind_cricket/)
